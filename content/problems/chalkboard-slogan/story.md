## Situation

A café owner writes a new slogan on the chalkboard every morning. Regulars get a free cookie when the slogan reads the same backwards as forwards. Spaces, punctuation and capital letters don't count, so **"Top spot"** qualifies.

## Goal

Decide whether today's slogan earns the cookie: answer True or False.

## Rules

- Only letters and digits count ([[alphanumeric]] characters). Spaces and punctuation are ignored.
- Capital and small letters are treated as the same letter.
- Read the board where it is: no copying the slogan onto a second board.
- An empty board reads the same both ways.

## Worked example

"Top spot" without its space and capitals is "topspot". Backwards it is "topspot" again, so the answer is **True**. "Top shop" becomes "topshop", which reads "pohspot" backwards: **False**.

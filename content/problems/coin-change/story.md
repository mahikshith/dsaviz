## Situation

A shop counter has coins of **1, 2 and 5**, as many of each as needed. A customer is owed **11**.

## Goal

Return the fewest coins that add up to exactly the amount, or −1 if it cannot be done.

## Rules

- Any coin may be used any number of times.
- The total must be exact.

## Worked example

**5 + 5 + 1** makes 11 with **3** coins. With only 2s, 3 cannot be made: **−1**.

## Situation

After a storm the village needs a rain barrel. Two builders will use two planks from an old fence as its walls. The planks stand in a row with heights **1, 8, 6, 2, 5, 4, 8, 3 and 7**, one unit apart.

## Goal

Find the most water any pair of planks can hold. The water rises to the shorter plank, and the barrel is as wide as the gap between the two planks.

## Rules

- Any two planks can be the walls; the planks between them do not matter.
- Water held = height of the shorter wall × the distance between the walls.
- Planks stay where they are.

## Worked example

Planks at positions 1 and 8 are 8 and 7 high, 7 apart. Water rises to 7, so they hold 7 × 7 = **49**, the most of any pair.

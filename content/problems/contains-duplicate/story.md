## Situation

A small party has a host who suspects a gatecrasher slipped in twice. Every guest wears a numbered badge, and five guests arrive wearing **3, 8, 4, 6 and 8**.

## Goal

Answer True if any number shows up more than once, and False otherwise.

## Rules

- Numbers arrive in any order.
- Seeing the same number twice is enough; how many times does not matter.
- An empty party has no repeats.

## Worked example

In 3, 8, 4, 6, 8 the number 8 arrives twice, so the answer is **True**. In 1, 2, 3, 4 every number is different: **False**.

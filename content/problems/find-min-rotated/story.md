## Situation

A staircase had steps rising in height, but it was cut at one point and the top piece was moved to the start: **3, 4, 5, 1, 2**.

## Goal

Return the height of the lowest step.

## Rules

- Before the cut, heights strictly increased.
- The cut may be anywhere, or nowhere.
- Aim for far fewer than n looks.

## Worked example

In 3, 4, 5, 1, 2 the staircase drops from 5 to **1**, the lowest step.

## Situation

Carol singers walk down a street of houses that have promised donations of **1, 2, 3 and 1**. Neighbours dislike hearing the same carols twice, so the group never sings at two neighbouring houses.

## Goal

Return the largest total of donations the group can collect.

## Rules

- No two neighbouring houses may both be visited.
- Any other selection of houses is allowed, including skipping many.
- Donations are non-negative.

## Worked example

Singing at the first and third houses collects 1 + 3 = **4**, the most possible.

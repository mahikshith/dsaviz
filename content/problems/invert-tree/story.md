## Situation

A garden is laid out like a family tree: the head gardener tends bed **4**, with beds **2** and **7** below, and so on: **4, 2, 7, 1, 3, 6, 9** floor by floor.

## Goal

Rearrange the garden into its mirror image, as if seen from the other side.

## Rules

- Every gardener's left and right beds swap.
- The swap happens on every floor, not just the top.

## Worked example

The garden 4 / 2 7 / 1 3 6 9 becomes **4 / 7 2 / 9 6 3 1**.

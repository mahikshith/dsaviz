## Situation

A talent show scores six acts: **3, 2, 1, 5, 6, 4**. The judges want the score of the **2nd best** act, and they only have a small room for the best acts.

## Goal

Return the k-th largest score.

## Rules

- Equal scores count separately: in 5, 5, the 2nd best is 5.
- 1 ≤ k ≤ number of acts.

## Worked example

The best scores are 6 and then **5**, so the 2nd best is 5.

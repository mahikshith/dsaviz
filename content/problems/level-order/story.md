## Situation

A treehouse is built in floors. The top floor has room **3**; below it are rooms **9** and **20**; below 20 are rooms **15** and **7**.

## Goal

List the rooms floor by floor, from the top, left to right within each floor.

## Rules

- Each floor becomes one list.
- Left before right within a floor.

## Worked example

The answer is **[3], [9, 20], [15, 7]**.

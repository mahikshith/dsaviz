## Situation

People stand in a line with heights **10, 9, 2, 5, 3, 7, 101, 18**. You may pick any of them, keeping their order in the line, to form a staircase of strictly rising heights.

## Goal

Return the largest number of people a staircase can have.

## Rules

- Picked people stay in their line order but need not be next to each other.
- Heights must strictly increase.

## Worked example

**2, 3, 7, 18** is a staircase of 4. No staircase of 5 exists.

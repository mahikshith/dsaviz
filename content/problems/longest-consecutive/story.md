## Situation

A diary's pages were torn out and scattered. You pick up pages numbered **100, 4, 200, 1, 3, 2**.

## Goal

Return the length of the longest run of consecutive page numbers you have.

## Rules

- A run is pages x, x + 1, x + 2, … with nothing missing.
- The pages are in no particular order and may repeat.

## Worked example

Pages **1, 2, 3, 4** form a run of **4**. 100 and 200 are runs of 1.

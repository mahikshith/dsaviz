## Situation

In an echo chamber, a word is painted across the wall: **racecars**. Standing in front of any letter, or between two letters, you shout; the echo spreads both ways as long as the letters on each side match.

## Goal

Return the longest stretch of the word that reads the same forwards and backwards.

## Rules

- The stretch must be consecutive letters.
- Centres can be on a letter or between two letters.
- If several stretches share the longest length, any is acceptable; the examples have one.

## Worked example

Shouting at the e in **racecars** echoes out to **racecar**, 7 letters. In **cbbd**, the echo from between the b's gives **bb**.

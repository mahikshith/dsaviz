## Situation

A hiker logs the height gained or lost on each leg of a trail: **−2, 1, −3, 4, −1, 2, 1, −5, 4**.

## Goal

Return the largest total of any stretch of consecutive legs.

## Rules

- A stretch is one or more consecutive legs.
- Totals can be negative; at least one leg must be chosen.

## Worked example

Legs 3 to 6 total 4 − 1 + 2 + 1 = **6**; no other stretch does better.

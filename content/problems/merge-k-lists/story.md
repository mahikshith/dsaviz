## Situation

Three sorted trains pull into one train yard: **1, 4, 5**, **1, 3, 4** and **2, 6**. A dispatcher must send all carriages out in one sorted train.

## Goal

Return a single train containing every carriage, in increasing order.

## Rules

- Each incoming train is already sorted.
- Some trains may be empty.

## Worked example

The yard sends out **1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 5, 6**.

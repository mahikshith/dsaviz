## Situation

Two walkers start at the front of a train of carriages **1, 2, 3, 4, 5**. One walks one carriage at a time; the other runs two at a time.

## Goal

Return the train starting from the middle carriage. With two middle carriages, use the second.

## Rules

- You can only walk forwards along the couplings.
- Walk the train only once.

## Worked example

For 1 to 5 the middle is **3**, so the answer is 3, 4, 5. For 1 to 6 it is **4**: 4, 5, 6.

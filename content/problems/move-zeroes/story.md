## Situation

A librarian tidies a shelf where some slots are empty. Reading left to right, the slots hold **0, 1, 0, 3, 12**, where 0 is an empty slot and other numbers are book IDs.

## Goal

Rearrange the shelf so all books come first, in their original order, followed by all the empty slots.

## Rules

- Books must keep their order.
- Use the same shelf: no second shelf.
- Empty slots are written as 0.

## Worked example

**0, 1, 0, 3, 12** becomes **1, 3, 12, 0, 0**.

## Situation

An explorer scans a map drawn as a grid, with **1** for land and **0** for water. Land cells that touch up, down, left or right belong to the same island.

## Goal

Return how many separate islands are on the map.

## Rules

- Diagonal neighbours do not connect.
- The edges of the map are surrounded by water.

## Worked example

In the second map the land forms three patches, so the answer is **3**.

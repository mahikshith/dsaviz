## Situation

Four people pose for a group photo holding the numbers **1, 2, 3 and 4**. Each person's score is the product of everybody else's numbers.

## Goal

Return every person's score, without using division.

## Rules

- A person's own number is left out of their score.
- Division is not allowed, because a 0 would break it.

## Worked example

Scores are **24, 12, 8, 6**: for example, the person holding 2 scores 1 × 3 × 4 = 12.

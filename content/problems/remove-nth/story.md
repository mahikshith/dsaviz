## Situation

A conductor must unhook the **2nd carriage from the end** of a train of carriages **1, 2, 3, 4, 5**. They can only walk from the front, and the train is too long to count twice.

## Goal

Return the train with the n-th carriage from the end removed.

## Rules

- Counting from the end starts at 1: the last carriage is 1st from the end.
- n is at least 1 and at most the number of carriages.

## Worked example

The 2nd carriage from the end of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 is 4. The train becomes **1, 2, 3, 5**.

## Situation

You stand on the right balcony of a treehouse and look in. Rooms hidden behind a room further right on the same floor cannot be seen. The treehouse is **1 / 2 3 / _ 5 _ 4**.

## Goal

List the room you can see on each floor, from top to bottom.

## Rules

- On each floor, you see only the rightmost room.
- A floor's rightmost room may hang under a left branch, if the right branch is shorter.

## Worked example

Floor 1 shows **1**, floor 2 shows **3**, floor 3 shows **4**.

## Situation

A square floor of painted tiles, numbered **1 to 9** in three rows, must be turned a quarter turn clockwise, but there is nowhere to put the tiles down meanwhile.

## Goal

Rearrange the tiles in place so the floor looks turned a quarter turn clockwise.

## Rules

- The floor is square, n by n.
- Rearrange in place, without a second floor.

## Worked example

Rows 1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 become **7 4 1 / 8 5 2 / 9 6 3**.

## Situation

People walk past a café window one at a time, with heights **1, 3, −1, −3, 5, 3, 6, 7** (measured from a reference line, so some are negative). The window fits **3** people at a time.

## Goal

Each time the window is full, report the tallest person in view.

## Rules

- The window holds exactly k consecutive people and slides by one.
- Report one value per window position.

## Worked example

The windows hold (1, 3, −1), (3, −1, −3), (−1, −3, 5), and so on; their tallest are **3, 3, 5, 5, 6, 7**.

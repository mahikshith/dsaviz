## Situation

You are packing a picnic and can bring any of three snacks: **1, 2 and 3**. You want to see every possible basket before choosing.

## Goal

Return every different basket, from the empty one to the one with every snack.

## Rules

- Each snack is either in the basket or not.
- The order of snacks inside a basket does not matter, nor the order of baskets.
- The snacks are all different.

## Worked example

Three snacks give **8** baskets: none; 1; 2; 3; 1 and 2; 1 and 3; 2 and 3; all three.

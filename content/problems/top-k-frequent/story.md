## Situation

A radio station logs song requests: **1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3**, where each number is a song. The DJ wants the **2** most-requested songs.

## Goal

Return the k songs requested most often, in any order.

## Rules

- The answer is unique in the examples: there is no tie for the last place.
- Songs are whole numbers.

## Worked example

Song 1 has 3 requests and song 2 has 2, so the answer is **1 and 2**.

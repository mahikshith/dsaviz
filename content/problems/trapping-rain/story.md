## Situation

Rain falls on a row of rocks of heights **0, 1, 0, 2, 1, 0, 1, 3, 2, 1, 2, 1**. Water settles in every dip between taller rocks and runs off the two ends.

## Goal

Return the total amount of water trapped above the rocks.

## Rules

- Each rock is one unit wide.
- Water above a rock rises to the lower of the tallest rock on its left and the tallest on its right.
- Water spills off both ends.

## Worked example

In the example, 6 units of water are trapped. With **4, 2, 0, 3, 2, 5**, the water rises to 4 across the middle: **9** units.

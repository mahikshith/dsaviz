## Situation

A robot stands in the top-left square of a floor **3 squares tall and 7 wide**. It can only move one square right or one square down at a time.

## Goal

Return how many different routes lead to the bottom-right square.

## Rules

- Moves are only right or down.
- Routes differ if their sequences of moves differ.

## Worked example

A 3 × 2 floor has **3** routes. A 3 × 7 floor has **28**.

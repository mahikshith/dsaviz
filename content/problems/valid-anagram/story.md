## Situation

Two friends each hold a bag of letter tiles. One bag spells **listen**, the other spells **silent**. They want to know whether either bag could spell the other's word.

## Goal

Answer True if the two bags hold exactly the same letters, the same number of times each, and False otherwise.

## Rules

- Order does not matter; only which letters and how many.
- Nothing may be left over in either bag.
- Words use lowercase letters a to z.

## Worked example

**listen** and **silent** both hold one each of e, i, l, n, s and t, so the answer is **True**. **rat** and **car** do not: the first has a t, the second a c.

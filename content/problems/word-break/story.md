## Situation

A fence is painted with one long string of letters: **applepenapple**. A sign-writer wants to cut it into words from a dictionary of **apple** and **pen**.

## Goal

Answer True if the whole string can be cut into dictionary words, and False otherwise.

## Rules

- Words may be reused any number of times.
- Every letter must belong to exactly one word.

## Worked example

**apple + pen + apple** uses every letter: **True**. **catsandog** cannot be split with cats, dog, sand, and, cat: **False**.

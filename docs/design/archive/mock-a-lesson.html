Dictionaries

Lesson 22 of 27 · about 6 minutes

  1. prices = {"apple": 3, "pear": 5, "plum": 4}
  2. print(prices["pear"])
  3. prices["fig"] = 7
  4. print("kiwi" in prices)

  • "apple"3
  • "pear"5
  • "plum"4
  • "fig"7

What you will learn

  • A dictionary stores pairs of a key and a value.
  • Looking up a key returns its value straight away.
  • Check with in before you look up.

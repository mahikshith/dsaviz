# CS50x (cs50.harvard.edu) - platform research

## Method
- Tool: REA MCP `capture_browser_scenario` (launch mode, Edge, DOM capture), date 2026-10-24.
- robots.txt: none (the path returns a 404 page). No bot check, no login.
- Pages: `/x/` (course home), `/x/weeks/3/` (week 3 as a sample), `/x/license/`.
- **Licence shown on the site: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).** Because DSAMaxxing is monetised, NonCommercial applies: facts and structure only, no copying of notes, slides, problem sets or videos.

## Summary and business model
- Harvard's introductory computer science course, presented as a free OpenCourseWare version plus paid verified tracks. Free path: work through 11 weeks, submit problem sets and a final project for feedback via an edX account. Paid options named on the page: verified certificate and professional certificates via edX, and credit from Harvard Extension School or Summer School. Donate link in the header.
- Extras: CS50.ai (AI help), Ed Discussion Q&A, VS Code in the browser, a "Gallery of Final Projects", lectures dubbed to Portuguese and Spanish, a family of sibling courses (CS50 Python, SQL, Web, AI, 2D, Business, Cybersecurity, R, Scratch, for Lawyers).

## Sitemap / URL patterns
- `/x/` home, `/x/weeks/<n>/` (n = 0 to 10, plus `/x/weeks/ai/`), `/x/notes/<n>/`, `/x/sections/<n>/`, `/x/psets/<n>/`, `/x/shorts/<name>/` (e.g. binary_search, merge_sort), `/x/project/`, `/x/practice/`, `/x/puzzles/`, `/x/seminars/`, `/x/license/`, `/x/syllabus/`, `/x/faqs/`, `/x/certificate/`, `/x/gallery/`, `/x/staff/`.
- Sibling courses live at `/python`, `/sql`, `/web` and so on.

## Features and how they work
- **Weekly page** (Week 3 Algorithms): a short description line ("Searching, sorting, asymptotic notation, recursion"), then tabs or lists for Lecture (audio, notes, slides in Google Slides or PDF, source code index, subtitles in English, Portuguese, Spanish, transcripts, video in English, Portuguese, Spanish with several resolutions, SDR and HDR), then **Shorts** (one short clip per concept: linear search, binary search, bubble sort, selection sort, call stacks, recursion, merge sort), Section, and Problem Set.
- **Course home** has a two-step teaching workflow for self-learners and an invitation for teachers to adopt the materials per the licence.

## Content coverage (counts)
- 11 weeks (Week 0 Scratch, 1 C, 2 Arrays, 3 Algorithms, 4 Memory, 5 Data Structures, 6 Python, 7 SQL, 8 HTML/CSS/JavaScript, 9 Flask, 10 The End), plus an Artificial Intelligence lecture, additional practice, final project and seminars. Week 3 is the algorithms week and has 7 "Shorts" (one short clip per concept), the closest match to our one-idea-per-animation style.

## Design language
- Bootstrap plus a bootstrap-table stylesheet and a custom `page.css`; Font Awesome icons from `fa.cs50.io`; MathJax for notation.
- Look: plain, document-like page, top bar with course name and links, left menu of weeks, many grouped media links. Minimal motion.

LeetCode {{P.n}} · {{P.title}}

Night has fallen on the old rope bridge. Six lanterns hang in a row, already lined up from dimmest to brightest. The gate on the far side opens only when two lanterns shine together with a glow of exactly 10.

Lumi and Roo need to find which two — and tell the gatekeeper their positions, counting from 1. There is exactly one right pair, and no lantern can be used twice.

{{P.synopsis}}

Given a 1-indexed array of integers numbers that is already sorted in non-decreasing order, find two numbers such that they add up to a specific target. Return their indices as [index1, index2].

Exactly one solution exists. You may not use the same element twice, and your solution must use only constant extra space.

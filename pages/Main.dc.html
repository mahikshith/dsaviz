Same step. Your depth.

One moment from the Lantern Bridge: the lanterns hold 1 and 11, the gate wants 10. Switch depth any time — the animation stays put.

Kid

“1 + 11 = 12. That's too bright! The only way to get dimmer is for Roo to swap to a dimmer lantern — so Roo steps left.”

Builder

total = 12 > target. Every pair that uses 11 is at least 1 + 11, so index 5 can never be in the answer. right -= 1.

Expert

nums[L] is the window minimum ⇒ nums[i] + nums[R] ≥ 12 > t for all i ∈ [L, R). Column R is dominated; discard it in O(1). Invariant: any answer lies in [L, R].
